📚 Measuring a harmonic component

For a real trigonometric polynomial $f$, define sine coefficients $A_n$ and cosine coefficients $B_n$ for integers $n\geq1$.

🖋️ Harmonic analyzers
{{{
A_n=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}f(t)\sin(nt)\,dt,\qquad B_n=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}f(t)\cos(nt)\,dt
}}}

📖 Orthogonality
Distinct positive integer sine modes have zero integral product over a full period. Each sine mode has squared integral norm $\pi$.

❓ Reading a coefficient
For $f(t)=3\cos t+4\sin t$, orthogonality gives $A_1=4$ and $B_1=3$; all coefficients with $n>1$ vanish.

📚 Verify the projection

🔏 The mixed integral vanishes
Since $2\sin t\cos t=\sin(2t)$, integrating over $[0,2\pi]$ gives $\int_0^{2\pi}\sin t\cos t\,dt=0$.
